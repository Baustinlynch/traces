# Callouts

> [!note] Note callout
> This is a note with a couple of lines.
> Second line of the note.

> [!info] Info callout
> Plain information here.

> [!warning] Watch out
> Warning body with **bold** and `code`.

> [!tip] A practical tip
> Tips go here.

> [!success] 
> Success callout with no title text (defaults to the type name).

> [!question] Question
> Anyone know the answer?

> [!danger] Danger zone
> Do not touch.

> [!error] Error callout
> Something failed.

> [!bug] Bug callout
> There is a bug here.

> [!example] Example callout
> An example of an example.

> [!quote] Quoting
> "Obsidian supports quote callouts."

## Plain blockquote (not a callout)

> Just a normal quote, no `[!` marker.

## Nested content inside a callout

> [!warning] Multi-line warning
> - item one
> - item two
> - [ ] unchecked item
