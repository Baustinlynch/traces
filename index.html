Traces is a "you ship, we ship" program for Hack Clubs: design and simulate real circuits in Wokwi, right in the browser, no breadboard, no soldering iron, no shipping delays. Just you, a schematic, and a blinking LED that actually works.

Bring Traces to your club See how it works ↓

A circuits YSWS, built for clubs.

Most YSWS programs ship you a kit and say good luck. Traces is different, it's made specifically for Hack Club - Clubs, so a whole club can learn circuit design together, at the same time, without waiting on the mail for parts.

Everything happens inside Wokwi, a free in-browser simulator for Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi Pico, and more. Wire up resistors, sensors, displays, and microcontrollers, hit run, and watch the simulation light up instantly. Mess it up? Undo and try again, nothing actually smokes up.

  • No parts to buy or ship
  • Runs in any browser, on any laptop
  • Real schematics, real code, real debugging
  • Built around your club's own meeting schedule

You choose the route the current flows.

Traces isn't one fixed curriculum, it's a menu. As a club, you and your members pick which workshops to run and in what order. Run one a meeting, binge three in a weekend, or pick just the ones your members are curious about.

01

Club leader picks the menu

Browse the workshop list, see what gear (if any, usually none) and time each one needs, and line up the ones that fit your club.

02

Members vote on what's next

Got a club full of robotics kids and a few who just want blinking lights? Mix and match, everyone can be working on something different.

03

Build it live, in Wokwi

Each workshop ends with a working simulated circuit your members designed themselves, ready to share, remix, or build for real later.

04

Ship it, get rewarded

Log your club's progress and submit finished projects the same way you would for any other YSWS, and get rewarded with club prizes or grants to actually build your demos!
